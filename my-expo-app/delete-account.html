How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion directly from the app or by contacting us.

Option 1: Delete from the App

  1. Open the TN HappyKids app.
  2. Go to Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Option 2: Request via Email

Send an email from your registered email address to dineshwebdev2003@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered name and phone number.

What happens when you delete your account:
  • Your profile and login credentials will be permanently removed.
  • Your associated data (attendance, fees, homework records) will be anonymized.
  • You will lose access to all app features immediately.
  • This action is irreversible.

Data Deletion & Retention

Upon account deletion:

  • Immediately deleted: Personal profile, login credentials, preferences, device tokens.
  • Anonymized within 30 days: Attendance records, fee history, academic progress (kept for school records but disassociated from your identity).
  • Retained for legal compliance: Any data required by applicable laws will be retained for the mandated period and then securely deleted.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
